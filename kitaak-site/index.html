How Kitaak works

Music When You Want It. Communication When You Need It.

Kitaak is designed to create a seamless audio experience for modern workplaces.

🎵

Enjoy Music

Listen to music while you work and create a more enjoyable atmosphere throughout the day.

💬

Stay Connected

Communicate with colleagues through Kitaak's communication features — chat, groups, voice and video.

🔊

Intelligent Audio Priority

When a call or voice communication begins, Kitaak automatically lowers the music so you can clearly hear the conversation.

🎶

Automatic Music Adjustment

When the conversation ends, the music smoothly returns to its preset level — without requiring you to constantly adjust the volume. Kitaak adjusts the music not only around conversations inside Kitaak but also around many other PC and mobile alerts, so nothing important gets drowned out.

The Kitaak experience

Your Workplace. Your Conversations. Your Music.

Workplaces are full of conversations, ideas, and moments that require attention. At the same time, the right music can help create a more enjoyable and welcoming environment.

Kitaak is built to help balance both. Instead of forcing users to constantly pause, stop, or manually adjust their music, Kitaak is designed to intelligently adapt the audio experience around communication.

The result is simple:

Less interruption.More connection.Music that works around your conversations.
Live communication

Never Miss an Important Conversation.

Communication should always have priority. With Kitaak, music is designed to adapt when voice communication takes place. Whether you receive a call or participate in a conversation, the audio experience adjusts to help keep communication clear.

When the conversation is over, your music experience continues.

Using the settings for Live Instant Communication, you can grant instant access during work hours to the individuals that have priority for live instant voice-over to reach you.

Because you shouldn't have to choose between enjoying music and staying connected.
