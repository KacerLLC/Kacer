How a conversation takes priority

Three moments, one rule.

The music plays

Your library, your playlist, your volume — on every device you are signed in to.

Someone talks

A call rings, a voice message arrives, or a live speaker starts in one of your groups. The music ducks automatically.

The music returns

When the conversation is over, the volume rises back to your preset — no buttons, no interruptions.

Runs everywhere

Desktop, web and mobile.

Android, iPhone, desktop and web versions are being built first. The Windows desktop version and the web version are the most likely to be available at the first release in March 2027; mobile versions and versions for other operating systems will follow later.
